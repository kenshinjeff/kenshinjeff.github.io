PhotoUP privacy policy

PhotoUP is a personal desktop utility used only by its developer on their own computers.

Google user data accessed

  • Google Photos, add-only access (photoslibrary.appendonly): to upload image and video files from folders the user selects and add them to one album PhotoUP created.
  • Google Photos, app-created data read access (photoslibrary.readonly.appcreateddata): to confirm that album exists and to check the result of uploads PhotoUP made. PhotoUP cannot see any other photos.
  • Email address (openid, email): to record which Google account each computer is signed in to.

Storage and sharing

All data stays on the user's own computers. Sign-in tokens are encrypted with Windows Data Protection for the signed-in Windows user. PhotoUP has no server, sends nothing to anyone other than Google's APIs, does not sell or share data, and does not use Google user data for advertising or for training any model.

Deletion

Remove access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Uninstalling PhotoUP and deleting its data folder removes all locally stored data. PhotoUP never deletes photos from Google Photos or from the computer.

PhotoUP's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
